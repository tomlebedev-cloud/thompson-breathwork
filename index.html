Practice safety and sources

For guided sessions, sit or lie down and breathe without straining. If you feel dizzy, short of breath, or uncomfortable, stop and return to normal breathing. Avoid breath holds and intensive practices in or near water and while driving. If you have a heart or lung condition, talk to a healthcare professional before trying these exercises.
